Precision Protocol Synergy: Integrating Cold, Pressure & Heat

While each modality delivers proven standalone benefits, our clinical edge comes from structured protocol sequencing. Pairing sub-zero vasoconstriction (-130°C Cryotherapy) with dynamic pneumatic flushing (Compression) and deep cellular hyperthermia (Infrared Sauna) creates a powerful vascular pump that rapidly clears metabolic waste and accelerates muscular repair.

Every session is custom-calibrated by our on-site physiologists according to your current training volume, biomarker readiness, and upcoming physical demands.

Whole-Body Cryotherapy

Exposure to temperatures reaching -130°C for up to 3 minutes. This extreme cold triggers a systemic survival mechanism, forcing blood to the core where it is heavily oxygenated and enriched with enzymes.

Session Duration: 20- 45 Minutes
Primary Benefit: Relief & Recovery

Compression Therapy

Utilizing dynamic sequential compression to effectively mimic the muscle pump of the legs and arms. This drastically enhances lymphatic drainage and clears metabolic waste (like lactic acid) post-exertion.

Primary Benefit: Metabolic Clearing

Infrared Sauna

Unlike traditional saunas, full-spectrum infrared light penetrates deeply into joint and muscle tissue. This induces a deep, detoxifying sweat at the cellular level while relaxing muscles and improving circulation.
