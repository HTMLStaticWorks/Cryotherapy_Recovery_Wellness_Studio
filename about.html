Why Cryotherapy

Why Choose Cryotherapy?

Faster, drier and more comfortable than an ice bath, with results you can feel after a single three-minute session.

Just 3 Minutes

A full systemic cold exposure in less time than a warm-up. No changing into wet gear.

Dry, Not Wet

Dry air at -130°C chills the skin without soaking deep muscle, so you leave without lasting stiffness.

Clinically Supervised

Every session is pre-screened and monitored by trained staff with real-time vitals.

Train Again Sooner

Reduced soreness and inflammation mean shorter gaps between hard training days.

The Process

How It Works

From first visit to measurable recovery, in four simple steps.

Book & Screen

Reserve online, then complete a short health screening with our clinical team.

Gear Up

We provide gloves, socks and slippers. Remove any jewellery and moisture from your skin.

Enter the Chamber

Two to three minutes at -110°C to -130°C, guided by a technician from start to finish.

Recover & Track

Warm up naturally, then log each session and how you feel in your member dashboard.
