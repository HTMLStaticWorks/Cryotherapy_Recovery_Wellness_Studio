The Mechanism

The Science of Cold

Physiological adaptation triggered by extreme temperature exposure aids in reducing systemic inflammation and accelerating muscular repair.

Circulation & Oxygen

Vasoconstriction followed by rapid vasodilation flushes toxins and delivers highly oxygenated blood directly to recovering tissues.

Inflammation Reduction

Cold exposure naturally suppresses inflammatory cytokines, mitigating delayed onset muscle soreness (DOMS) post-exertion.

Endocrine Response

Triggers the release of endorphins and norepinephrine, boosting mood, mental clarity, and metabolic function.
